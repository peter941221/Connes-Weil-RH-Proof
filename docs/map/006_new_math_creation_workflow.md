# 006 — New-math creation workflow

Status: binding process authority subordinate to [003] and the core-progress
gate in `RH_MAINLINE_FREEZE.md`.

This workflow exists to generate proofs of the selected-detector sign, not to
accumulate interfaces around it.

## Entry contract

Every campaign starts with a same-owner ledger containing:

```text
owner:       exact CompactLog test / operator / finite prime set
consumer:    theorem that will use the result
target:      one explicit inequality or summability statement
hypotheses:  every currently assumed premise
remove:      the one premise this campaign will prove
failure:     a checkable counterexample, lower bound, or stop condition
```

If no healthy B5 consumer can be named, do not start the campaign.

## Creation loop

1. **Normalize the wall.** Expand definitions until the missing statement is
   a scalar inequality, integral estimate, spectral tail, or operator-energy
   bound on the committed owner.
2. **Generate mechanisms.** Propose identities, decompositions, comparison
   principles, or compactness arguments that act on that exact object.
3. **Screen cheaply.** Check parity, scaling, support, sign orientation,
   dimensions, endpoint behavior, and known no-go hypotheses before writing
   Lean.
4. **Price the mechanism.** State constants, quantifiers, uniformity, and the
   exact source of each margin. An unnamed `C`, `large enough`, or equivalent
   hypothesis is not a producer.
5. **Prove the smallest decisive brick.** Prefer a bound that removes one
   current premise, or a counterexample that kills the mechanism.
6. **Verify on the real consumer.** Rewire the existing theorem, check axioms,
   and show the old placeholder path is inactive.
7. **Update the map only on a route-level change.** Routine theorem chronology
   belongs in `MEMORY.md` and `docs/proofs/`.

## Admission tests

A candidate survives only if all are true:

- same healthy `CompactLog` owner as the selected detector;
- exact support-dependent finite prime set is preserved;
- no assumption of `qw >= 0`, `SourceRH`, RH, or an equivalent coverage
  statement;
- no replacement of the selected detector by an arbitrary nearby test without
  a proved stability theorem;
- no use of the normalized additive-convolution owner as producer;
- the proposed result removes a named open premise rather than renaming it.

Numerics may locate a constant, test a sign, or generate exact certificate
data. They do not change route status until a rigorous proof or reproducible
counterexample is recorded.

## Required outcome

A substantive campaign must end with at least one of:

1. an unconditional bound with an explicit margin on the actual owner;
2. a named reproducible no-go under stated hypotheses; or
3. a strictly smaller quantitative obligation with a previous premise proved.

An equivalent reformulation, new `MasterExit`, coordinate transport, or
conditional wrapper is not a successful loop.

## Mechanism registry

Only these classes remain live under the current route ruling:

- signed physical-kernel / C3' cancellation on the selected finite owner;
- phase-balanced two-span spectral negativity with explicit tail margin;
- ROOT-local endpoint bricks that name a direct semi-local consumer.

The G8/R3 operator-energy program is retained in [012] and [042] as a fallback
normal form, but generic work there is frozen unless it directly discharges
the selected-detector sign consumer.

## Kill ledger

For every rejected mechanism record, in one place:

```text
mechanism / owner / failed hypothesis / evidence / scope of no-go
```

Do not generalize a scoped failure beyond its hypotheses. Conversely, do not
reopen a mechanism while those hypotheses are unchanged. Current binding
examples are [089] and [093].

## Provenance

Distinguish literature, inspiration, project derivation, and formal proof.
Candidate original mathematics must keep dated derivations and verification
evidence, followed by prior-art review. The workflow cannot turn a borrowed
argument into an original claim by repackaging it.
