# 040 — R3 diagonal trace-limit energy constraint

**Authority:** supporting.

**Status:** formal necessary condition and conditional limit. A finite real
limit of either actual-cutoff diagonal trace forces square-summability of the
uncut same-owner detector-root columns on the same source basis. Conversely,
that square-summability hypothesis suffices for convergence to the uncut
root-energy trace. The required estimate, hence the unconditional diagonal
limits, remains open.

**Consumer:** the selected-owner G8 ledger for detector-specific semi-local
B5 positivity on a healthy `CompactLog` carrier; the binding route in
[003](003_b1_b5_minimal_exit_route_selection.md) is unchanged.

The proof is recorded in [1485](../proofs/1485_r3_diagonal_trace_limit_energy_constraint.md).
The necessary direction combines pointwise convergence of the actual-cutoff
root columns with the finite-cutoff trace-energy identity from
[039](039_r3_actual_cutoff_diagonal_root_energy.md). The converse direction
uses the fixed-pair strong-sandwich limit theorem for the literal G8 cutoff.
Therefore the exact remaining diagonal obstacle is the same-owner
root-column square-sum estimate; pointwise strong convergence alone does not
provide it. The leakage/common-right square-sum obligations, remainder
decay, endpoint/P2 sign, `qw` readback, C3, and RH remain open.
