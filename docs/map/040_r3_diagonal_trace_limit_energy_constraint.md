# 040 — R3 diagonal trace-limit energy constraint

**Authority:** supporting.

**Status:** formal necessary condition. A finite real limit of either
actual-cutoff diagonal trace forces square-summability of the uncut
same-owner detector-root columns on the same source basis. The diagonal
limits themselves remain open.

**Consumer:** the selected-owner G8 ledger for detector-specific semi-local
B5 positivity on a healthy `CompactLog` carrier; the binding route in
[003](003_b1_b5_minimal_exit_route_selection.md) is unchanged.

The proof is recorded in [1485](../proofs/1485_r3_diagonal_trace_limit_energy_constraint.md).
It combines pointwise convergence of the actual-cutoff root columns with the
finite-cutoff trace-energy identity from [039](039_r3_actual_cutoff_diagonal_root_energy.md).
Thus an eventual finite trace limit cannot be inferred from pointwise strong
convergence alone: it needs a genuine same-owner square-sum estimate. The
result supplies no such estimate and no diagonal or total trace limit. The
leakage/common-right square-sum obligations, remainder decay, endpoint/P2
sign, `qw` readback, C3, and RH remain open.
