Date: 2026-09-17

compresses the four-branch B4 estimate to two collective objects:

`outer + reflected-outer`

and

`second-support - prolate`.

If the two corresponding source-basis square sums are finite after bounded
surrounding factors, their sum is the full source commutator square sum.  The
signed second/prolate pairing is retained, so this consumer does not replace a
potential cancellation by separate absolute estimates.

single compact-root owner and the second-support/prolate remainder is kept
coupled.  The physical-factor estimates needed to instantiate these two
premises remain open; B4, S3, G8, and C3 are not closed.
