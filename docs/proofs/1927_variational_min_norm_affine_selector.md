## Formal result

`ConnesWeilRH/Dev/C1VariationalFiniteDimensionalSelector.lean` proves
`exists_min_norm_preimage`:

```text
L : E ->ₗ[ℂ] F,   x : E,   E finite-dimensional Hilbert

there exists z with L z = L x and
  for every w, L w = L x, ||z|| <= ||w||.
```

The proof identifies the affine fibre with `x + ker L`, applies the complete
subspace minimum theorem to `ker L`, and transports the distance minimum back
to the original norm. The finite-dimensional completeness input is explicit;
it is not supplied by an axiom or by a stored witness.

`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`; no `sorryAx` occurs.

## Route-A meaning

The existing `windowedFiniteMellinVector_span_top` theorem already proves that
the finite Mellin target data are generated by a finite-dimensional span of
actual windowed tests. The new theorem therefore removes one genuine premise
from the next selector design: “choose an arbitrary representative of the
Mellin interpolation fibre.” A future selector may instead minimize a norm
representing the physical derivative cost on a finite source basis.

This is not yet that physical-cost estimate. In particular, the theorem does
not prove that the ordinary Hilbert norm equals the derivative seminorm, nor
that the minimizing representative has a uniform cost in the selected owner
parameters. Those are the next explicit obligations.
