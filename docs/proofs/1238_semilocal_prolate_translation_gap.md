# 1238 - Semilocal prolate/Sonin translation gap

Date: 2026-09-09.

Status: `LITERATURE-BACKED GAP`; no Lean declaration, numerical run, or RH
claim is opened by this record.  The consumer is the binding healthy-
`CompactLog`, selected-detector, same-owner B5 exit from map record

## 1. What the cited literature actually supplies

The Connes--Consani archimedean construction uses a Sonin-space positive
trace.  In their notation the relevant functional is of the form
`Tr(theta(g) S theta(g)^*)`, where `S` is the projection associated with the
simultaneous support/vanishing conditions.  The same paper explains the
``move Delta inside Sigma'' step through a pair of projections and prolate
functions, and controls the bad direction by a rank-one estimate.  This is an
archimedean window result, not an explicit finite-visible-prime owner for the
current orbit detector:

* [Connes--Consani, *Weil positivity and Trace formula, the archimedean
  place* (arXiv:2006.13771)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13771).

The later prolate-wave-operator paper gives a semilocal framework and points
to a semilocal prolate operator as a future route.  Its stated program is to
find the second (finite-prime) candidate and establish the needed spectral
control; it does not provide the completed same-owner positive kernel and
`qw` trace identity required by L4/A4 here:

* [Connes--Consani--Moscovici, *Zeta zeros and prolate wave operators*
  (arXiv:2310.18423)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18423).

These are positive mechanisms and route evidence, not a drop-in proof of the
remaining project obligation.

## 2. Exact translation obligations for this repository

To use the literature mechanism on the active branch, one formula must carry
all of the following data on one healthy `CompactLog` owner:

1. the orbit detector and its finite visible-prime set;
2. the multiplicative/log-coordinate support projection and the finite-prime
   shadow projection (the semilocal analogue of the Sonin/prolate pair);
3. an internal, cutoff-dependent correction whose whole kernel is positive;
4. a finite-cutoff trace identity whose terms are the actual same-detector
   archimedean and visible-prime contributions;
5. a limit theorem reading that trace back to `qw g`.

The existing formal leaves provide pieces of this interface, but not the
analytic owner in item 2 or the positive factorization in item 3.  Records
[`1236`](1236_canonical_internal_identity_counterterm_no_go.md) and
[`1237`](1237_window_to_response_defect_no_go.md) additionally rule out the
canonical identity-complement correction and the tempting vanishing-defect
repair.  Thus the literature narrows the search to a genuinely semilocal
prolate/Sonin operator, rather than licensing another wrapper around the
current projection response.

## 3. Decision

The direct literature-import path is closed at the translation boundary.  The
next admissible mathematical brick is an explicit semilocal prolate owner
(or an equivalent two-channel positive block kernel) with the five readbacks
above.  Until that formula exists on paper, no new Lean interface and no
fixed-rank numerical campaign should be opened.  This is not a no-go theorem
for a future operator-valued construction; it is a precise reason that the
current G6 candidate remains `NEEDS-ANALYSIS`.

RH is not claimed.
