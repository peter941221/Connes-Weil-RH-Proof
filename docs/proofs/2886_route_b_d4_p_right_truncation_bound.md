# 2886 Route B D4 right truncation bound

For `|t| <= 1/100`, the order-20 Taylor polynomial from record 2885 obeys the
exact rational bound

`P_truncated / t^2 >= 37.1540416118... > 0`.

This proves positivity of the truncated polynomial on the small right endpoint
neighbourhood. The order-22 and higher Taylor remainder is deliberately not
included; a full endpoint sign certificate still needs that remainder bound.
