# Record 1528: outer-pair non-commutation expansion

Date: 2026-09-17

The bridge leaf now proves two exact facts.  First, the source inclusion is
fixed by the actual archimedean Fourier-support projection, using the second
component of the defining intersection subspace.  Second, for arbitrary
ambient factors `Q` and `M`, the signed outer pair expands after source
inclusion as

`E Q E M J - E Q M J + E M Q J - M E Q J`.

The final term retains `E` between `M` and `Q`; it cannot be replaced by
`M Q J` without an additional `E`/`Q` compatibility theorem.  Thus a generic
two-term radial-leakage reduction is not valid.  The result is formal and
does not supply a square-sum estimate.  WO-B remains open at the atomic
mixed-factor estimates, with the non-commuting four-term expansion now
explicitly exposed.
