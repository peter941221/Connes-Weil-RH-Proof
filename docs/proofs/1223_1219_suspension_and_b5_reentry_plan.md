## 2. Re-entry choice

The viable route is the windowed projection-cutoff positive-trace owner, not
another absolute defect budget and not a ROOT-to-orbit density lift.  At every
cutoff it already has a positive trace-class operator

```text
P_n = C_n† K_S C_n,
```

and its formal contract is exactly

```text
remainder_n -> 0,
Re Tr(P_n) - remainder_n -> qw(g).
```

Positive traces then force `0 <= qw(g)` by the existing B5 consumer.  This
keeps the detector, its finite visible-prime owner, and the target `qw` on one
healthy `CompactLog` owner.

Record 1211 rules out the tempting fixed-response choice: a vanishing insertion
defect plus a bounded fixed response contradicts the cofinal trace growth for a
nonzero detector.  A viable response must contain the same linear cutoff bulk;
the object to construct is therefore a moving finite-part / renormalized
response together with its exact counterterm ledger.  Operator-norm decay of
the insertion sandwich is useful but is not a trace-convergence proof.

## 3. Required sequence

1. **Counterterm specification, before new numerics or Lean work.**  Register
   a candidate response `R_n(g)` and scalar counterterm `B_n(g)` with the
   exact intended readback
   `Re Tr(P_n) - B_n(g) - Re Tr(R_n(g)) -> qw(g)`.  It must identify the
   linear bulk coefficient from the existing cutoff-trace ledger, rather than
   postulating its cancellation.
2. **Admission falsifier.**  On a nonzero selected owner, prove or compute
   the finite-cutoff trace identity and check that the candidate response has
   the same leading bulk.  A bounded response, a zero counterterm, or a
   mismatch in that coefficient is an immediate no-go under record 1211; no
   large exact-Fraction grid is justified.
3. **Same-owner algebraic bridge.**  If the admission check survives, add the
   minimal active-C1 ledger that identifies the finite part with the existing
   archimedean and finite-prime readback.  The target is the two fields of
   `ProjectionCutoffLimitContracts`, not a universal all-test sign theorem.
4. **Analytic convergence.**  Prove the renormalized remainder tends to zero
   and separately establish the compressed-kernel compatibility used for the
   insertion sandwich.  Both estimates must be on the selected orbit detector
   and must not assume `qw >= 0` or an equivalent gate sign.
5. **Only then compose.**  Build the focused owner/audit pair and invoke the
   existing B5 `SourceRH` exit.  No B1 campaign, normalized additive owner,
   or ROOT partition lift is permitted.
