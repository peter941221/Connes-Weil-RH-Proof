# 2782 representative D4/D7 budget sandwich

For each derivative order 4 and 7, the sum of the two child-cell norm integrals is nonnegative and is bounded above by the exact representative-cell rational width times the corresponding endpoint maximum.

This is the owner-correct budget interface for later signed-kernel accounting. It does not transfer the endpoint-only charge bound and does not prove `qw >= 0` or RH.
