# 2817 Route B exterior power tail

This record combines the exterior power majorant with Mathlib's exact
improper integral formula.  For exponent `a < -1`, it bounds the norm of the
tail integral by `C * (-T^(a+1)/(a+1))`.

The row-B constants and the D4/D7 numerical instantiation remain explicit
future premises.  No cardinal pins, SourceRH, or RH claim is made.
