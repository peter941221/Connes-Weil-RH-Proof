# Proof 763: Canonical-system entropy and the relative Gate mismatch

Date: 2026-08-02

Status: primary-source applicability audit and corrected successor contract.
The available real-line Szego theorems control the entropy of one spectral
measure through one canonical Hamiltonian.  They do not compare the source and
finite-Euler endpoints, retain the compact-root detector, or identify the
completed outer/reflected-second-support/prolate response from Proof 761.

This closes the proposed direct Arov-gauge import negatively.  It does not
reject a new source-owned relative canonical system whose detector derivative
is first proved to be the canonical Gate scalar.  Gate 3U, the finite-S sign,
the arithmetic same-object identity, Burnol's identity, and RH remain open.

## 2. What the three source theorems say

### 2.1 Even diagonal Hamiltonians

Bessonov--Denisov start with an even spectral measure and a diagonal
Hamiltonian

```text
H(t)=diag(h_1(t),h_2(t)).                              (CS.2)
```

Their Theorem 1 characterizes membership of that one measure in the real-line
Szego class by finiteness of the nonnegative block-oscillation functional

```text
K(H)=sum_n [
  (integral_(eta_n)^(eta_(n+2)) h_1)
  (integral_(eta_n)^(eta_(n+2)) h_2)-4
].                                                    (CS.3)
```

It also gives two-sided exponential comparisons between `K(H)` and the
normalized entropy of the same measure.  It is a characterization, not a
relative endpoint identity.

Primary source:

```text
R. V. Bessonov and S. A. Denisov,
A spectral Szego theorem on the real line,
Theorem 1,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05671
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2019.106851
```

### 2.2 General canonical Hamiltonians in PdB gauge

The later Bessonov--Denisov theorem treats a general singular nontrivial
`2 x 2` Hamiltonian.  Its coefficient functional is again a block
mean-oscillation quantity `K(H)`.  Theorem 1.2 states

```text
spectralMeasure(H) is in Sz(R)  <->  K(H)<infinity,   (CS.4)

c_1 K_m <= K(H) <= c_2 K_m exp(c_2 K_m),             (CS.5)
```

where `K_m` is the entropy of the one Weyl function generated by `H`.  The
paper explicitly notes that the exponential comparison is essentially sharp.
It supplies neither a difference `K(H_S)-K(H_0)` nor a derivative in a
detector direction.

Primary source:

```text
Roman Bessonov and Sergey Denisov,
De Branges canonical systems with finite logarithmic integral,
Theorem 1.2,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.05622
https://doi.org/10.2140/apde.2021.14.1509
```

### 2.3 Exact Arov-gauge sum rule

Damanik--Eichinger--Yuditskii consider the two-dimensional canonical system

```text
partial_t mathcalA(z,t) j
  =mathcalA(z,t)(-i z A(t)+B(t)),                     (CS.6)

A(t)>=0,
A(t)+B(t) upper triangular,
tr B(t)=0.                                           (CS.7)
```

The last two conditions are the Arov gauge.  For the single Schur spectral
function `w` associated with this system, Theorem 2.1 is the exact identity

```text
I(w)=integral_0^infinity
  [tr A(t)-2 sqrt(det A(t))] dt.                     (CS.8)
```

If `lambda_1(t),lambda_2(t)` are the eigenvalues of `A(t)`, the integrand is

```text
(sqrt(lambda_1(t))-sqrt(lambda_2(t)))^2.             (CS.9)
```

Thus `(CS.8)` is a positive anisotropy energy for one A-gauge canonical
system.  Gauge freedom preserves the spectral Schur function; it does not
introduce a second Euler-weighted endpoint or a compact-root observable.

Primary source:

```text
David Damanik, Benjamin Eichinger, and Peter Yuditskii,
Szego's theorem for canonical systems: the Arov gauge and a sum rule,
Theorem 2.1 and Appendix B,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.03267
https://doi.org/10.4171/JST/371
```

## 5. Scale guard from the actual Schur algebra

Proof 418 supplies an exact `2 x 2` local guard.  For one Euler amplitude
`a`, its normalized physical response and canonical Euler charge have scales

```text
q_a=2 c a/(1+a^2),
I_a=-log(1-a^2),

q_a/I_a -> infinity,
q_a/sqrt(I_a) -> 2c.                                 (CS.12)
```

Therefore no relative construction may claim a local estimate linear in the
quadratic Arov/Szego charge.  The scale-compatible target is a global
Cauchy--Schwarz estimate.  A square root of the Euler energy is sufficient for
Gate 3U because Proof 416 already bounds the full canonical energy by a
polynomial in the selected support radius.

## 6. Correct relative producer

A successful canonical-system successor must construct, on the literal
Proof 761 owner, two measurable coefficient fields

```text
J_(owner,t)  -- detector/root boundary field,
C_(owner,t)  -- relative Euler coefficient field,      (CS.13)
```

and prove all three statements below.

First, the same-object readout:

```text
Re FullCompletedKernelTrace_(canonicalFamily owner)
  =integral Re <J_(owner,t),C_(owner,t)> dt.           (CS.14)
```

Second, the detector energy bound:

```text
integral ||J_(owner,t)||^2 dt
  <=P_root(owner.supportRadius)
    ||owner.sourceTest||_(H^r)^2.                     (CS.15)
```

Third, the relative coefficient bound:

```text
integral ||C_(owner,t)||^2 dt
  <=C * canonicalEulerSquareEnergy(owner).            (CS.16)
```

Then one legal Cauchy--Schwarz step gives

```text
abs(Re FullCompletedKernelTrace)
  <=sqrt(P_root) ||sourceTest||_(H^r)
    sqrt(C * canonicalEulerSquareEnergy),             (CS.17)
```

which has the required support-polynomial scale.

Equations `(CS.14)--(CS.16)` are not definitions to be assumed.  In
particular, `(CS.14)` must display all three physical branches and establish
the trace cycle before `(CS.15)` or `(CS.16)` is used.  This is the exact
`relative Arov coefficient response` contract left by the source audit.

## 7. Minimal-flow determinant boundary

A nearby 2025 result constructs a semifinite determinant for Toeplitz
operators associated with a compact minimal flow.  Its Theorem 6 places smooth
symbol semicommutators in the semifinite trace ideal, and Theorem 22 computes a
multiplicative-commutator determinant from the invariant-mean bulk cocycle.

```text
Efton Park,
A determinant formula for Toeplitz operators associated to a minimal flow,
Theorems 6 and 22,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04207
```

This does not supply `(CS.14)`.  The finite Euler logarithm is almost periodic,
so it has a compact translation hull.  A nonzero compact-root detector
multiplier is in `C_0(R)` by the Riemann--Lebesgue lemma, while

```text
AP(R) intersect C_0(R)={0}.                            (CS.18)
```

Indeed, a nonzero almost-periodic function has relatively dense approximate
returns and cannot tend to zero at both infinities.  Thus the localized
detector is not a symbol on the compact Euler hull.  Extending by an invariant
mean kills the `C_0` direction and records only the bulk cocycle, precisely the
normal-ordering datum that Proofs 422--425 show is insufficient.

Park's relative determinant framework may still be useful after a
Burnol-specific boundary ideal is constructed.  The cited theorem does not
construct that ideal or identify its derivative with Proof 761.
