# 2816 Route B exterior power integrability

This record proves that a function dominated on `(T, infinity)` by
`C * x^a`, with `a < -1`, is Bochner-integrable there.  It is the
integrability premise required by the improper-tail consumer.

The theorem does not instantiate the row-B coefficient bounds and does not
claim a numerical tail value, cardinal pins, SourceRH, or RH.
